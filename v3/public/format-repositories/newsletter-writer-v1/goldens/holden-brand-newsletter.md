## Body

Before Holden offered more than 500,000 customizable products, R.B. “Pete” Holden was making three-ring binders in a small Dallas parking garage.

That was 1978.

The easy move would have been to keep selling what already worked. But in 1992, as business moved toward a paperless future, Holden adapted and entered the promotional products industry. The Holden family also became actively involved, beginning the transition into a second-generation family-owned business.

We kept adapting. Holden built its own software platform to improve the customer experience and make the work behind it more efficient. Along the way, Texas Instruments named Holden Vendor of the Year for product quality and customer service, then recognized us again in 2004 and 2006.

But a long history only matters if it changes what you get today.

That is the Holden difference. You get access to branded products, custom apparel, packaging and kitting, and corporate gifts without being left alone with a giant catalog. We work with you every step of the way to find a solution that fits your brand, audience, and goal.

Your brand is not one size fits all. Your service should not be either.

Nearly five decades have taught us when to adapt, where the details matter, and how to keep a big project personal.

Have an idea in mind—or no idea where to start? Let’s make a game plan and bring your brand to life.

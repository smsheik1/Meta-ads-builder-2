Overnight conclusion

The next win is not more templates. It is better Maker correction and stricter truth.

What was fixed

  • OpenRouter analysis is pinned to the healthier W&B Gemma 4 31B provider.
  • The analysis prompt names the exact allowed binding values.
  • A brand-bound news subject is no longer mistaken for the brand logo.
  • Brand-level Formats no longer force a product choice; product-image Formats still do.
  • The remix prompt now locks to the selected product and forbids unsupported reports, ratings, events, and offers.

What still blocks the assistant

  • SAM can miss an important image and leave it baked into the locked background.
  • There is no simple Maker correction step for adding, cropping, replacing, or rebinding that missing image.
  • Prompt instructions do not guarantee factual copy. Exact claims still need machine-checkable evidence.
  • NIM generation can take 60–120 seconds, time out, or fail at the network.
  • The 196-item product dropdown is technically usable but not assistant-friendly.

Bigger ideas that need approval

  • A Maker asset checklist with “crop from reference,” upload, image search, fixed/changeable, and reroll-group controls.
  • A deterministic evidence validator that rejects unsupported product facts and source-brand leakage before showing an ad.
  • Durable generation jobs with visible stages and a manual retry button that preserves completed work.

The three improvements to make next

  1. Let the Maker correct missing images. This unlocks the news inset, ranked thumbnails, platform logos, and other formula-critical art.
  2. Check every generated claim against real evidence. Stop fake counts, ratings, reports, tester programs, and off-product copy before render.
  3. Make product choice searchable and reliable. The assistant should find one product quickly, and every output must use exactly that product.

# Daily 3D Breakdown Brand-Ad Series

## Series objective

Make one polished 20-second 3D Breakdown ad each day for:

> Making ads for my favorite brands until I get hired or funded.

Each video should be good enough to publish, preserve the official Wiggly 3D Breakdown recipe, and deliberately improve one part of the format over the previous day's video.

## Chat contract

This task is reserved for planning, making, rendering, reviewing, publishing, and improving this daily 3D Breakdown video series.

In scope:

- brand and product selection;
- evidence-backed brand research;
- story directions, scripts, captions, and post copy;
- storyboard, endpoint, video, voice, render, and inspection work;
- production tooling or fixes required to finish these videos;
- backlog, scheduling, retrospectives, and quality improvements for the series.

Hard rule: if the user asks for work outside this scope in this task, do not perform it. Respond:

> FUCK OFF — [specific reason the request is outside the daily 3D Breakdown video series].

## Daily definition of done

A day is complete only when:

1. Official website research supports every factual claim.
2. Five story directions are saved and one direction is intentionally selected.
3. The script, CTA, scene, and six-frame plan pass validation before paid media.
4. The storyboard and all four full-quality production endpoints are individually reviewed.
5. Clip 1 is approved before clip 2 is generated.
6. The final video is rendered through `AdRenderSurface`.
7. The final MP4 is 1080×1920, 20 seconds, and contains one narration stream.
8. The finished video is watched beside FinalStraw and at least one other reference.
9. The LinkedIn post copy is ready.
10. One concrete lesson is recorded for the next day's improvement target.

## Queue policy

- Work from the top unless the user deliberately reprioritizes the backlog.
- Produce one brand per day.
- Do not start the next brand until the current video has a final creative review.
- A render that passes technical checks but is not worth posting remains in progress.
- Provider retries require a stated failure reason and fresh approval.
- Preserve each completed run, evidence package, and retrospective so improvements compound instead of resetting.
